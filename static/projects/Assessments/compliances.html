Confidential Project "Compliances"

Collaborated within a cross-functional team to develop an automated framework for assessing organizational hardware against ISO standards. I was primarily responsible for designing and automating over 200 end-to-end (E2E) test cases, ensuring that device commands and compliance checks met strict regulatory and functional requirements.

My main activities involved:

  • Working closely with Product Owners, Designers, and Business Analysts to validate User Stories and define clear Acceptance Criteria for device assessments.
  • Architecting and automating 200+ end-to-end test scripts that execute diagnostic commands and evaluate device health against ISO compliance benchmarks.
  • Managing and maintaining detailed test documentation and execution reports using Allure TestOps to provide transparent results to stakeholders.
  • Verifying that developed features met all business and security standards, providing the final sign-off on approved functionalities.
  • Tracking testing cycles and reporting on the overall health of the device battery, ensuring all defects were identified and addressed before deployment.
